# NYC Kids with Special Needs: Resource Dashboard

A plain-language, parent-built guide to navigating developmental disability services in New York City, compiled while going through the process firsthand.

## What's covered

- **Special Education Schools** — the CSE/IEP process, plus public, state-funded, and private school options
- **Evaluations** — where to get a psychological, psychosocial, or neuropsychological evaluation (free, Article 16, and private-pay options)
- **Education Attorneys & Advocates** — NYC-area attorneys and non-attorney advocates for CSE disputes and impartial hearings
- **OPWDD Application & Enrollment** — a step-by-step path through eligibility, the Front Door, Medicaid/HCBS Waiver, Care Manager, and Self-Direction
- **Community Activities & Providers** — arts, sports, social-skills groups, therapy/medical providers, ABA providers, and camps, crowdsourced by parents

Every resource is labeled by how it's paid for: free, self-pay, private insurance, Self-Direction/Fiscal Intermediary reimbursable, or state-funded placement.

## Updating the page

The whole site is a single self-contained file, `index.html`. To publish a change, edit that file and upload it to this repo (or drag-and-drop a new version over the old one on GitHub); GitHub Pages redeploys automatically within a minute or two.

## About this page

Compiled by a parent while going through the OPWDD application, evaluation, and school-placement process, to save other families some of the searching, drawing on official state and city sources plus community-shared lists. It's offered as a starting point, not legal, medical, or benefits advice. Always confirm current requirements, costs, wait times, and insurance/Medicaid acceptance directly with each organization, and with OPWDD, your DDRO, a Care Coordination Organization, or your school's CSE, since program details and web addresses do change.

This page is not indexed by search engines (see the `noindex` meta tag in `index.html`); it's meant to be found by people you share the direct link with.
